Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version
  • Your sets and takes stay in your own browser unless you choose to make an AI video from one.
  • We keep your account (email, name, plan, videos left) so you can sign in and use what you paid for.
  • We never sell your data and never show you ads.
  • A few services help us run HandCam; each only gets what it needs (listed below).

Who we are

HandCam (handcam.onrender.com) is operated by Ayush Dhanraj Bhiogade, an individual based in India ("we", "us"). Contact: ayucorp1304@gmail.com.

Cookies and storage

One sign-in cookie (hc_session) keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Supabase keeps your sign-in session in your browser's storage. We also remember small preferences (like the frame you shoot in) on your device. No advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep things

Your account and purchase history: while you have an account, and afterwards as long as tax and accounting rules require. AI videos and their inputs: while your account exists, or until you ask us to delete them. Takes in your browser: until you delete them or clear your browser's data.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete your data, or to close your account, by emailing ayucorp1304@gmail.com. We'll answer within 30 days. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a plan on its own: cancel it first (Account → Cancel plan) so you aren't billed again. Payment records may need to be kept for tax law.

Children

HandCam isn't for children under 13 (or under 16 where the law requires). We don't knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are handled by Supabase and never stored in plain text; your own AI key is encrypted. The accounts database only accepts our server's secret key. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, we'll tell you.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell signed-in users in the app.
