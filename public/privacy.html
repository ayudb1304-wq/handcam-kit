Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version
  • Your videos, photos, logos, sets and takes are yours, and private to your account.
  • We don't use them to train AI models, and we never show them publicly without your written OK.
  • Free trial videos are deleted 14 days after they're made. On a paid plan your videos are kept while you have the plan, and for 30 days after it ends.
  • You can download your data, delete any video, delete all your videos or delete your account yourself, in Account → Your data.
  • We never sell your data and never show you ads. A few services help us run CineHand; each only gets what it needs (listed below).

Who we are

CineHand (cinehand.com) is operated by Ayush Dhanraj Bhiogade, an individual based in India ("we", "us"). Contact: sushi@cinehand.com.

Your content is yours

  • Private. Your videos and everything they were made from can be opened only from your account. The app gives nobody else, us included, a way to browse another account's videos.
  • Not used for training. We don't use your videos, photos, logos, sets or takes to train AI models, and we don't sell or share them for that.
  • Never shown publicly without you. Examples on our site are our own, or shown with the maker's written permission.
  • When we would look. We open a customer's content only to fix a problem that customer has asked us about, or when the law requires it.
  • The AI services. Higgsfield (and the video model it runs) and Anthropic receive only what is listed above, and handle it under their own terms.
  • People in your pictures. Only upload photos you have the right to use. If a product photo has people in it, crop them out first.

Cookies and storage

One sign-in cookie (hc_session) keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. One device cookie (hc_dev, a random number, up to 2 years) and a scrambled form of your network address are kept with your account, only so the free trial videos are given once per device and network. Supabase keeps your sign-in session in your browser's storage. We also remember small preferences (like the frame you shoot in) on your device. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices and rights

In the studio, under Account → Your data, you can do these yourself, at once:

  • Download my data: a file with your account, plan, history of charges, purchases and the list of your videos.
  • Delete a video: the ✕ next to it in Videos.
  • Delete all my videos.
  • Delete my account: your videos, your saved sets and takes, your plan and videos left, your saved AI key and your sign-in. Cancel a running plan first (Account → Change plan → Cancel plan) so you aren't billed again.

You can also ask us to show you, correct or delete anything by emailing sushi@cinehand.com. We'll answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under your local privacy law (for example the GDPR in Europe or the DPDP Act in India), including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

CineHand isn't for children under 13 (or under 16 where the law requires). We don't knowingly collect their data.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. We never see or store a password: you sign in with Google. Your own AI key is encrypted. The database and the file storage only accept our server's secret key: no browser can read them directly. Every video is checked against the account asking for it. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, we'll tell you.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell signed-in users in the app.
